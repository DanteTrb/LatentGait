| S6 Figure S1 / subject contrasts | reporting.contrasts | aggregate_subject_contrasts.json | Paired latent-score (Z) and probit-location (tau) posterior draws; participant-specific hypothetical contrasts summarized only in aggregate. The primary `lower_tail_90` convention orders observed baseline latent-instability scores increasingly, weights each by the absolute posterior-mean contrast for Δs = 0.3, combines tied scores, and extends from the minimum observed score to the first score at which the cumulative fraction reaches or exceeds 90%. The actual included fraction is reported. The optional `central_90` rule is not used for the primary Figure S1 summary. This cumulative interval is descriptive, distinct from the derivative-based high-sensitivity region, and not a biological threshold, clinical target, or causal treatment effect. Exact numerical agreement with the historical Figure S1 boundaries remains unverified; no individual-row export. |

SMDs are descriptive faller-minus-nonfaller differences divided by sqrt((SD_faller^2+SD_nonfaller^2)/2); binary sex uses the analogous pooled Bernoulli variance. This convention is explicit because the manuscript table does not specify its denominator. Degenerate denominators remain missing rather than being fabricated.

`run --figures` also generates private plots from the live fitted distributions: measurement score/uncertainty distributions, primary adjusted-logistic probability, functional-form/derivative/contrast panels, and the cumulative subject-contrast distribution. These plots are generated from the run, not a graphical replica of manuscript artwork or a claim of numerical agreement. No graph is drawn from stored paper results. The source's conceptual workflow figure does not require fitted data and is described in the README. All plots remain outside Git.
